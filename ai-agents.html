How AI agents could change enterprise workflows

A practical starting point for leaders and professionals exploring this topic.

Think in end-to-end journeys

An agent can help retrieve information, draft recommendations or coordinate steps. The value comes from improving the entire journey, not adding an isolated chatbot.

Connect the systems of record

ERP, CRM and service platforms hold important business context. Integrations, data quality and permissions determine whether an agent can contribute safely.

Keep humans in control

Define which tasks an agent may perform independently, which require approval and how exceptions are escalated. Start with narrow, observable workflows.
