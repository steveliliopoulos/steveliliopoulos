FOUNDATIONAL PERSPECTIVE

Who is accountable when AI supports decisions?

A practical starting point for leaders and professionals exploring this topic.

Accountability cannot be delegated to a model

Assign a business owner to each AI-enabled process. Make clear who approves deployment, monitors performance and handles errors.

Match controls to the decision

A low-risk drafting assistant needs different oversight from an agent changing customer records. Permissions, review and audit trails should reflect the consequences of failure.

Adoption is part of governance

Staff need to know when to use AI, how to verify its output and when to escalate. Training and feedback loops are as important as technical controls.

A practical question for leaders

If an AI-assisted decision is challenged tomorrow, can we explain what happened and who was responsible?
