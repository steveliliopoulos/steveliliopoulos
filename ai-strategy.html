Why AI Transformation Is a Business Transformation, Not a Technology Project

Making AI work for business starts with changing how work gets done.

Introducing AI tools and transforming a business with AI are two different things. The real opportunity is to improve the way people, processes, information and technology work together.

Explore why business problems should come before technology choices, how operating models shape results, and four practical questions leaders can use to get started.

Start with the outcome

Rather than begin with a long list of AI use cases, identify one important workflow where delays, hand-offs or disconnected information affect customers or employees. Decide what better performance would look like before choosing a tool.

Make the change measurable

Faster customer resolution, shorter reporting cycles and fewer avoidable errors are examples of outcomes worth testing. AI should support a better way of working, with people retaining clear accountability for decisions.
