Playbook 01 · AI adoption

Is your AI agent pilot ready to scale?

Start with one workflow and measure how often the agent’s work reaches the right outcome without someone having to redo it.

Run four checks before expanding

  1. Define the job. Name the cases the agent can handle, the outcome it should produce and the exceptions it must hand to a person.
  2. Measure a baseline. Sample comparable cases from the existing workflow. Record first-pass quality and staff time, including time spent correcting errors.
  3. Review outcomes. Agree what counts as correct before testing. Sample routine cases and exceptions. Track corrections, escalations and policy breaches separately.
  4. Agree the decision rule. Set thresholds with the process owner before the pilot. Give someone authority to pause the rollout and define what triggers that pause.

Worked example · Customer service triage

A service team pilots an agent that categorises and routes incoming enquiries. In this illustrative test, the agent routes 90 of 100 eligible enquiries correctly on the first pass. Staff correct the remaining 10. That’s a 90% first-pass routing rate. The team agreed to require at least 95% before expanding, so it reviews the misroutes and reruns the test after making changes.

The team also tests whether the agent hands sensitive enquiries to a person. It tracks correct handoffs separately and pauses expansion if any sensitive case bypasses review.

Use this checklist at your pilot review

  • We know which cases are in scope and who owns the workflow.
  • We can compare pilot results with the existing process.
  • We can show the number of correct outcomes, corrections and escalations.
  • We tested exceptions and know who can pause the rollout.
  • We have recorded the decision to expand, refine or pause, with a reason.
